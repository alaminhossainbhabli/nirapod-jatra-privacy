3. Security, Consent & Anti-Abuse Controls

  • Traveler Consent: Guardians can only view your status after you tap Allow. You can Reject or Block anyone at any time (3 rejections auto-block).
  • Brute-Force Protection: 5 invalid Pair Code attempts block the offending device. Unblocking requires an Admin review via alaminhossainbhabli@gmail.com.
  • On-Device Protection: Saved Pair Codes and SMS numbers are masked on-screen (********) and protected by fingerprint/device lock. Local data uses AES-256 EncryptedSharedPreferences (allowBackup="false").
  • Network & Integrity: All server traffic uses HTTPS/TLS (usesCleartextTraffic="false"), and the app checks cryptographic signature integrity to prevent tampered/modified builds.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Retention

Nirapod Jatra uses Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore) strictly to sync safety status and check app updates. Firebase may process data in international data centers under Google's Privacy Policy. Local data is erased when you clear data or uninstall the app.

5. Your Rights & Children's Privacy

You may revoke permissions in Android Settings, clear your SMS number, or stop watching inside the app anytime. For server-side deletion requests, email alaminhossainbhabli@gmail.com. This app is not directed to children under 13 without parental supervision.

6. Emergency Disclaimer & Terms of Use

Important Safety Notice: Nirapod Jatra is a personal safety companion tool and is not a replacement for official emergency services (such as 999, 911, or 112), police, or medical responders. Alert delivery depends on device battery, GPS signal, mobile network/SMS balance, and internet availability. Always contact local emergency authorities directly in life-threatening situations.
