Privacy Policy — Nirapod Jatra

Effective Date: November 23, 2026
Developer: Al Amin Hossain
Contact Email: alaminhossainbhabli@gmail.com

Summary: Nirapod Jatra is a 100% free, ad-free personal safety timer app. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

This Privacy Policy explains how Nirapod Jatra collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Android mobile application.

1. Information We Collect and Use

  • Location Data (Precise & Background Location): Your GPS/network coordinates are collected only when you actively start a safety journey or when an emergency timer expires without check-in. This allows your approved family guardian (and/or your saved offline SMS contact) to receive your location and Google Maps link in an emergency.
  • Anonymous User ID & 8-Digit Pair Code: We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication (an anonymous UID) and a randomly generated 8-digit Pair Code to link travelers with their approved family guardians and to enforce brute-force protection (blocking devices after 5 invalid code attempts). We do not collect your real name, email address, or personal profile on our servers.
  • Emergency SMS Phone Number (Optional): If you choose to save a family member's phone number for offline emergency SMS alerts, it is stored locally on your device inside AES-256 encrypted storage and is never uploaded to our servers.
  • Device Battery Level: Checked during an active safety journey so your approved guardian can be alerted automatically if your phone battery drops to 5% or below.

2. App Permissions Explained

  • Precise Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Needed to obtain accurate coordinates for your safety check-ins and emergency alerts.
  • Background Location (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION): Needed so live location updates and emergency alerts continue working reliably even when your phone screen is locked or the app is in the background during an active journey.
  • Send SMS (SEND_SMS): Used strictly on-device during an expired safety timer or low-battery emergency to send an offline SMS with your Google Maps link to your saved emergency number when internet is unavailable.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to display active journey status, connection alerts, and emergency alarms.
  • Exact Alarm & Foreground Service: Used to wake the device precisely when your safety timer ends and keep safety monitoring active during a trip.

3. Third-Party Services

Nirapod Jatra uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) solely to authenticate sessions anonymously, transmit live journey/emergency status to approved guardians, and check for app updates. You can review Google's privacy practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Data Security & Retention

  • All network communication with Firebase uses encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.
  • Local app preferences, Pair Codes, and optional emergency phone numbers are encrypted on-device using Android AES-256 EncryptedSharedPreferences.
  • We display no advertisements and use no third-party ad-tracking SDKs.
  • Trip status records are kept in Firestore solely for active safety functionality; locally stored data is permanently deleted if you clear app data or uninstall the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Nirapod Jatra is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us so we can address it promptly.

6. Your Control & Data Deletion

You can mark yourself safe, stop watching, or clear your saved emergency SMS number at any time inside the app. If you have questions about your data, want to request deletion of a server-side trip/block record, or need device unblock support, contact us at alaminhossainbhabli@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
