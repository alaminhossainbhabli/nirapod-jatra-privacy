Privacy Policy — Nirapod Jatra

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 | Contact: alaminhossainbhabli@gmail.com

Nirapod Jatra is a personal safety timer application designed to help travelers share emergency alerts and live safety status with trusted family members.

1. Information We Collect and Use

  • Location Data (Fine & Background Location): Collected only when you start an active safety journey or when an emergency timer expires, so your approved family guardian (or your saved offline SMS contact) can receive your coordinates and Google Maps link.
  • Anonymous Identifiers & Pair Code: The app uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication (an anonymous UID) and a randomly generated 8-digit Pair Code to connect you with your approved guardian and to prevent brute-force abuse (blocking devices after 5 invalid code attempts). We do not collect your name, email address, or personal profile.
  • Emergency SMS Number (Optional): If you save a family mobile number for offline SMS alerts, it is stored locally on your device using AES-256 encrypted storage and is never uploaded to our servers. SMS messages are sent directly from your device only during an emergency.
  • Battery Level: Used during an active journey to alert your approved guardian if your battery drops to 5% or below.

2. Data Security & Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) solely to transmit journey status and emergency alerts over encrypted HTTPS connections. Local preferences are encrypted with AES-256. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or third parties, and the app contains no ads.

3. Your Control & Data Deletion

You can stop watching, clear your saved SMS number, or mark yourself safe at any time inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally encrypted data. For any privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us at alaminhossainbhabli@gmail.com.
