This Privacy Policy & Terms of Use document explains how Nirapod Jatra ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, stores, protects, and deletes information in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, Samsung Galaxy Store Guidelines, and applicable global privacy laws.

1. Prominent Disclosure (Background Location & Emergency SMS)

  • Background Location Disclosure: Nirapod Jatra collects location data to enable live safety tracking and emergency SOS alerts only while a safety journey is active, even when the app is closed or not in use (screen locked or in background). Location data is never collected in the background when no journey is active, and is never used for advertising.
  • Emergency SMS Disclosure: In supported countries (Bangladesh, India, Pakistan, China), the App uses the SEND_SMS permission strictly on-device to send an emergency SMS with your Google Maps link to the family mobile number you explicitly saved, only if your safety timer expires without check-in or your battery drops to ≤5%.

4. User Consent, Privacy Controls & Anti-Abuse

  • Explicit Guardian Approval: Entering a Pair Code sends a watch request only. The traveler must tap Allow before any location or status is visible, and can Reject or permanently Block any requester (3 rejections trigger automatic blocking).
  • Brute-Force Protection: 5 consecutive invalid Pair Code attempts automatically block the device to prevent guessing. Unblocking requires manual Admin review via alaminhossainbhabli@gmail.com.
  • On-Device Privacy: Pair Codes and emergency SMS numbers are masked on-screen (********) and protected by device biometric/PIN/pattern authentication.

5. Security Measures & Data Retention

  • In Transit: All cloud communications strictly require HTTPS/TLS (usesCleartextTraffic="false").
  • At Rest: Sensitive local preferences are encrypted using Android AES-256 EncryptedSharedPreferences with cloud/USB backup disabled (allowBackup="false").
  • App Integrity: The App performs on-device root detection, debugger detection, and cryptographic signature verification to block tampered builds.
  • Retention: Local data stays on your device only until cleared or uninstalled. Cloud trip documents retain only the latest status and coordinates required for active safety functionality.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

Nirapod Jatra uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) solely to authenticate anonymous sessions, transmit real-time safety status, enforce security rules, and check app version updates. Firebase may process data on servers located internationally in accordance with Google's security standards. Review Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Data Deletion & User Rights (GDPR / CCPA / Global)

  • On-Device Deletion: Clear your saved SMS number or stop watching inside the App, or uninstall the App to permanently erase all locally encrypted data.
  • Server-Side Deletion Request: Because the App uses anonymous authentication with no email/account profile, you may email alaminhossainbhabli@gmail.com with your 8-digit Pair Code or Device UID to request deletion of any associated Firestore document.
  • Permission Withdrawal: You can revoke Location, SMS, or Notification permissions at any time in your Android device Settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Nirapod Jatra is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction) without parental supervision, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Contact us immediately if you believe a child's data requires removal.

Alert delivery depends on device battery, GPS accuracy, mobile signal, SMS balance, and internet connectivity. Always contact local emergency authorities directly in life-threatening situations.
