## ✨ Core Features

- **Exact Safety Timer:** Preset (`10m`, `20m`, `30m`, `60m`) and custom durations with reliable wake-up alarms and `HH:MM:SS` countdown.
- **Live Family Tracking:** 30-second GPS and battery status updates shared securely with an approved family watcher.
- **Low-Battery & Offline SOS:** Automatic emergency alert at `<= 5%` battery and offline SMS with a Google Maps location link.
- **Multilingual Support:** Available in 7 languages — English, বাংলা, हिन्दी, தமிழ், తెలుగు, اردو, and 中文.
